inbox-triage

inbox-triage is a personal tool that keeps a Gmail inbox clear of promotions and newsletters.

My family and I use it. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public.

What it does

Once a day it signs in to the account owner's Gmail and looks at the sender of each message in the inbox. It sorts the senders into five groups: receipts, alerts, newsletters, promotions, and everything else.

It labels and archives messages from newsletter and promotion senders, which moves them out of the inbox. Everything else stays where it is. It never deletes mail and never sends mail.

It also writes a short digest listing what it archived.

Why it asks for Gmail access

The tool connects to Gmail over IMAP, a standard protocol for reading and managing email. Labeling and archiving mail over IMAP requires Google's https://mail.google.com/ scope. A scope is a named permission an app asks Google for, and this is the only scope Gmail accepts for IMAP sign-in. The tool uses that access only to read message headers, apply labels, and archive.
