Michigan Fever, 1836

A family leaves a worn-out New England farm in the autumn of 1836 to buy government land in Michigan. They go by wagon to Albany, by canal boat to Buffalo, then by lake steamer or the Black Swamp road to Detroit, and west on the Territorial Road to the land office at Bronson. The land has to be paid for and a roof raised before winter, and after 15 December the land office takes only gold and silver.

The family is invented. The fares, distances, prices and diseases come from period guides and later histories, and every event carries a note on what the record says. Numbers the record does not give are invented for the game and listed with the reason.
